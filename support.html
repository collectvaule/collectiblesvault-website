Support

Need a hand?

Most questions are answered below. Anything else, email us directly and we'll get back to you personally.

🐞

Found a bug?

Email us a quick description of what happened and, if you can, what you were doing right before it — screenshots help a lot.

💳

Need a refund?

All purchases go through Apple's App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple, not us — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

🔒

Privacy question?

CollectiblesVault stores your collection only on your device — no account, no cloud. See the full breakdown on the Privacy page.

💡

Feature request?

We read every email. Tell us what you're trying to do and we'll see if it fits where the app is headed.

🔑

Trouble with API keys?

Auto-Value needs your own eBay and Anthropic keys entered in Settings. Double-check they're pasted in full with no extra spaces — that's the most common issue.

✉️

Anything else

Just email us. A real person reads and replies to every message.
