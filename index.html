What it does

Everything a real collection needs

Not just a spreadsheet with photos — a vault built around how collectors actually track and value what they own.

📷

Instant valuations from a photo

Snap a photo and Auto-Value identifies the item with AI, then prices it from current eBay listings — a live market snapshot. Prefer your own number? Enter it manually anytime.

Requires your own API keys, entered once in Settings, plus an internet connection: eBay's key is free, Anthropic's runs on prepaid credit you add to your own account (about 1–2¢ per scan). Manual tracking works with no keys and fully offline.

🗂️

Track anything you collect

Cards, figures, globes, framed memorabilia — anything. Organize by category, search instantly, and see your whole collection at a glance.

🔒

Locked behind Face ID

Your collection lives only on your device, behind a real vault door and Face ID — no account, no cloud database holding your data.

📈

Value-over-time charts

Watch your collection's worth change over time, broken down by category, with a ledger view built for collectors who track value seriously.

📡

Beam it to another collector

Share Nearby sends your live vault straight to another collector's phone — Bluetooth/Wi-Fi direct, no internet, no account required on either end.

🧾

Insurance-ready PDF reports

Generate a formatted, photo-included report for insurance or estate records — send it by mail, text, print, or save it to Files.
